# BHUMISETU (भूमि सेतु) — Executive Pitch & System Overview

## Problem Statement (SIH26019)
Infrastructure acquisition in India suffers from fragmented land records, boundary disputes, manual compensation awards under the RFCTLARR Act 2013, and ecological degradation from highway realignment through dense forest corridors.

## Proposed Solution
**BHUMISETU** is a Unified National Digital Land Intelligence Platform that bridges state land registries (Bhuiyan), spatial GIS GIS cadastral boundaries (Bhu-Naksha), AI-driven land cover change detection, and automated compensation calculation into an evidence-driven decision support system.

### Key Value Pillars
1. **Evidence-Based Governance**: Automated cross-validation of textual record-of-rights (Khasra P-II / Khatauni B-1) against satellite imagery.
2. **Accelerated Infrastructure Planning**: Cost-surface least-cost path routing that prioritizes barren (Banjar) land while avoiding irrigated farmland and reserved forests.
3. **Statutory RFCTLARR Compliance**: Automated calculation of Section 26-30 compensation awards (Market Value $\times$ Multiplier $+$ 100% Solatium $+$ Structure Allowances) with 1-click ready-map export.
4. **Forest Impact & Compensatory Afforestation Tracking**: Satellite canopy loss analysis and net NPV ecological valuation.

## Target Stakeholders
- **Government Revenue Officers & District Collectors**: Instant access to verified cadastral boundary maps, pending dispute litigation notices, and tamper-proof audit chains.
- **National & State Highway Authorities (NHAI/PWD)**: Corridor buffer impact analysis and cost-optimal alignment recommendations.
- **Citizens & Land Owners**: Transparent access to authenticated land extracts and compensation estimates.
